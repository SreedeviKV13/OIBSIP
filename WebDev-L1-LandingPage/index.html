Our story

"We wanted clothes that felt like they belonged to a place — not a season. So we started with the flax fields, not the fashion calendar."

Isolde began in 2019 as a single sewing table above a linen mill outside Amalfi. Every piece is still cut by hand from fabric woven within the region, using dyes made from olive leaf, madder root, and sea salt. We release two small collections a year — no more, no less — because that's what the flax harvest allows.

— Marguerite Voss, Founder
